Policies

Warranty, delivery, cancellation, and refunds — plain language, no fine print surprises.

Questions on any of this, or need to report a delivery issue? Reach us by email or WhatsApp (+234 705 460 0639).

Warranty

Warranty coverage is provided by the applicable manufacturer or distributor and may vary by product. Before we finalize your order, we'll confirm the warranty available for your selected product. Where required, our team can also help you connect with the relevant supplier for warranty support.

A product listing won't claim "warranty included" without saying what kind and how long — where we haven't confirmed that yet for a specific item, we'll say so rather than leave it vague.

Delivery

Delivery availability and charges depend on the product and destination. Your delivery cost and estimated timeline will be confirmed before we finalize your order — delivery available to selected locations initially, with other locations handled on request.

Orders are fulfilled through our supply partners or approved delivery providers. If your order arrives visibly damaged, please notify us within 24 hours of delivery with photos or videos of the product and packaging so we can investigate and assist with resolution.

Membership Cancellation

You can cancel your membership's renewal at any time. Cancelling stops your next subscription charge — it does not immediately remove access you've already paid for. Your membership stays active until the end of your current billing period.

To cancel renewal, email us at priceedgeio@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp — self-service cancellation from your account is on our roadmap but not built yet.

First Membership Guarantee (Refunds)

We want your PriceEdge membership to provide real value. If you're a new member and can't find a suitable deal for what you're looking to purchase, you may request a refund within 48 hours of your first membership payment, provided you haven't completed a purchase through PriceEdge.

This guarantee applies to your first membership payment only — it doesn't apply to renewals. Refund requests remain subject to verification of membership and account activity.
